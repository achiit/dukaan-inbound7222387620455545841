About the Shop

Hello, I am Neelam.

I opened this small shop in 2014 with one glass counter and a few lipsticks. My sister helped me carry the first stock home from Sadar Bazaar, and my customers were mostly the girls from our own gali.

Today women come to me before every wedding, Teej and college function. I still remember what each regular likes, and I will not sell you something that does not suit your skin.

Come in, try the shade on your hand, and take your time. There is no pressure here.
